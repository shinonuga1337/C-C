History of Love: Immortal

For years, Cecillion hid his true nature behind the mask of a celebrated opera singer in Avalor. Behind the applause and fame lived the last of the Blood Demons — lonely and haunted by his past.

One night, he met Carmilla, a noble lady whose grace and gaze awakened feelings he thought long lost. Their secret love bloomed under the moonlight, far from the eyes of the world.

But their happiness was destroyed by Count Ansaac, Carmilla’s father, who deemed Cecillion unworthy. Imprisoned and forced into a loveless marriage, Carmilla took her own life on her wedding day.

Heartbroken, Cecillion returned and revived her with his blood. From that night on, they became eternal — two souls bound by love, forever hiding from the world.

Moonlit Waltz
Duo Skill

Cecilion can activate the skill Moonlit Waltz, transforming Carmilla into a Phantom Shadow attached to him. While attached, Cecilion protects her from damage and gains a shield based on Carmilla's level. After activation, Carmilla can choose a destination to dash to, dealing magic damage to enemies in the area and briefly slowing them.

Tips

Use this skill to protect Carmilla, safely enter the battlefield, or initiate a fight. Synchronize Cecilion's activation with Carmilla's chosen position to maximize damage and crowd control.
